# PoE2 Crafting Guide: Vaal Orbs & Corruption

Source: mobalytics.gg (paraphrased for compliance with licensing restrictions)

## Overview

Vaal Orbs permanently corrupt an item, preventing ALL further currency use.
This means: no more crafting, no quality, no sockets, no divines. Ever.

**Rule:** Always complete all other crafting BEFORE using a Vaal Orb:
- Apply quality (Blacksmith's Whetstone / Armourer's Scrap)
- Fill mod slots (Exalted Orbs)
- Add sockets (Artificer's Orbs)
- Socket Runes/Soul Cores

## Vaal Orb Outcomes — Rare Items (25% each)

1. **No Change** — item just becomes corrupted (worst outcome)
2. **Chaos-like reroll** — applies Chaos Orb effect 1-3 times. Usually ruins the item.
3. **Add Enchantment** — special corruption-only enchantment. Often very good.
   Examples: +1-3% to max fire resistance, +50-100 accuracy, +1 to minion skill levels
4. **Add Extra Socket** — bypasses normal socket limit. On caster weapons: +/- quality (0-23% range)

## Vaal Orb Outcomes — Unique Items (25% each)

1. **No Change** — corrupted
2. **Add Enchantment** — same as rare items
3. **Add Socket / Quality change** — same as rare items
4. **"Krangledivine"** — Divine Orb effect, THEN multiply each mod by random 0.78-1.22 factor
   (45 equally likely values in 0.01 steps, rounded to nearest whole number).
   Can make items incredibly powerful (+22%) or terrible (-22%).

## Vaal Orb Outcomes — Skill Gems (mixed %)

- 25% No Change
- 12.5% Add support gem socket (good — like a free Greater Jeweller's)
- 12.5% Remove support gem socket (bad — basically destroys it)
- 12.5% +1 gem level enchantment (best outcome, retained on level up)
- 12.5% -1 gem level enchantment (bad, retained on level up)
- 25% Add/remove up to 10% quality (0-23% range)

## Vaal Orb Outcomes — Waystones/Maps (often worth it)

- 25% No Change
- 25% Tier +/-1 (primary method for tier 16 waystones)
- 12.5% Remove all prefixes, add suffix for each removed
- 12.5% Remove all suffixes, add prefix for each removed
- 25% Add mods beyond normal limits (best waystones)

Low risk / high reward on maps — only bad outcome is -1 tier.

## Vaal Orb Outcomes — Jewels

- 25% No Change
- 25% Chaos-like reroll (does nothing on unique jewels)
- 25% Add enchantment (Corrupted Blood immunity is strongest)
- 25% Add/remove random modifier. Unique jewels get krangledivined.

## Omen of Corruption

- Found from Ritual encounters
- Removes the "No Change" outcome (all rolls are impactful)
- Only use if item is worth ≥3x the Omen's trade value
- Math: 4 regular Vaals on 4 items = same positive outcomes as 3 Omens on 3 items

## Strategy

- Best used when you have TWO similar items — Vaal one, keep backup
- On gems: great value early when Greater Jeweller's Orbs are expensive
- On waystones: almost always worth it for the chance at extra mods
- On gear: only Vaal items you've fully finished crafting
- Free Vaal: Paquate's Mechanism in Act 3 (once per character + once in Cruel)

## Quality with Corruption

- Vaal Orb on caster weapons/gems can add/remove quality
- Range: 0% to 23% (above normal 20% max)
- 23% quality is the ceiling for corrupted items
