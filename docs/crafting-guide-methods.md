# PoE2 Crafting Guide: Methods & Currency

Source: mobalytics.gg (paraphrased for compliance with licensing restrictions)

## Currency Orbs — Crafting Flow

### Basic Crafting Chain
1. **Orb of Transmutation** → Normal → Magic (1 mod)
2. **Orb of Augmentation** → Magic (1 mod) → Magic (2 mods: 1 prefix + 1 suffix)
3. **Regal Orb** → Magic → Rare (adds 1 mod, unlocks up to 6 total)
4. **Exalted Orb** → Rare → adds 1 random mod (without removing)
5. **Divine Orb** → rerolls numeric values within tier range only

### Rerolling & Removing
- **Chaos Orb** → removes 1 random mod AND adds 1 new random mod (NOT a full reroll)
- **Orb of Annulment** → removes 1 random mod
- **Orb of Alchemy** → Normal → Rare with 4 mods directly (skips transmute/aug/regal)

### Greater/Perfect Variants
Greater and Perfect versions of orbs restrict to higher-tier mods only:
- **Greater Transmute/Augment**: only rolls mods requiring ilvl ≥ 44
- **Perfect Transmute/Augment**: only rolls mods requiring ilvl ≥ 70
- **Greater Regal/Chaos/Exalted**: only rolls mods requiring ilvl ≥ 35
- **Perfect Regal/Chaos/Exalted**: only rolls mods requiring ilvl ≥ 50

This dramatically narrows the pool and increases chance of high-tier results.

### Corruption (Vaal Orb)
Permanently prevents further crafting. Outcomes (25% each for rare items):
1. No change (just corrupts)
2. Chaos-like reroll 1-3 times (usually ruins item)
3. Add special enchantment (often good)
4. Add extra rune socket (exceeds normal limit)

Best used as final step after all other crafting is complete.

### Other Important Currency
- **Fracturing Orb** → locks 1 random mod permanently (fractured mod can't be removed)
- **Orb of Chance** → Normal → Unique OR destroys the item
- **Mirror of Kalandra** → copies a non-unique item (extremely rare)
- **Artificer's Orb** → adds a rune socket to martial weapon or armour

## Item Rarity & Mod Limits

- **Normal (white)**: 0 explicit mods
- **Magic (blue)**: max 2 (1 prefix + 1 suffix)
- **Rare (yellow)**: max 6 (3 prefixes + 3 suffixes)
- **Unique (orange)**: fixed mods, cannot be crafted on (mostly)

A Rare item with mods removed stays Rare — rarity just sets the cap.

## Mod Pool Mechanics

- Each item type has a specific mod pool based on its class and attribute tags
- Mods are divided into Prefixes and Suffixes
- An item cannot have two mods from the same mod family
- Hybrid mods count as one mod slot but provide two stats at lower values
- Tiers determine stat ranges — T1 is best, higher numbers are worse
- Item Level determines which tiers are eligible (higher ilvl = access to better tiers)
- ilvl 82+ unlocks all mod tiers for most items

## Weight System

- Each eligible tier has a spawn weight
- Probability = tier_weight / sum(all eligible tier weights in that affix pool)
- Higher tiers often have LOWER weights (rarer to hit T1 than T5)
- Greater/Perfect orbs narrow the pool by excluding low-level tiers

## Tags

Mods have tags (Life, Fire, Attack, Caster, etc.) that can be manipulated:
- Essences guarantee a specific tagged mod
- Omens can target specific tag categories
- Catalysts increase quality of tagged mods on jewellery
- Influence runes (Kolr's Hunt, Katla's Gloom, etc.) add separate tagged mod pools

## Crafting Strategy Summary

1. Choose a good base (high ilvl, correct attribute type for desired mods)
2. Use Transmute/Aug to fish for a key mod cheaply
3. Regal to Rare, hoping for a good second mod
4. Exalt to fill remaining slots (or Annul bad mods first)
5. Divine to perfect numeric values within tier ranges
6. Socket + Rune for final enchantments
7. Vaal (optional) for enchantment gambling as absolute last step

For expensive crafts: Greater/Perfect orbs to narrow pools, Essences to guarantee one mod, Omens for targeted prefix/suffix control.
