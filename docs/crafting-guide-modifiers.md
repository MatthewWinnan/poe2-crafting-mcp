# PoE2 Crafting Guide: Item Modifiers & Structure

Source: mobalytics.gg (paraphrased for compliance with licensing restrictions)

## Modifier Types

### Implicit Modifiers
- Exist on all items of that base type regardless of rarity
- Shown above explicit mods (e.g., Topaz Ring always has Lightning Resistance)
- NOT affected by crafting currency (Chaos, Alchemy, etc.)
- CAN be rerolled by specific means (Blessed Orb equivalent, Vaal Orb)

### Explicit Modifiers
- The craftable mods below the implicit
- Changed by currency orbs
- Divided into Prefixes and Suffixes

### Enchantments
- Shown above implicits in light blue text
- Added by Runes socketed in item, or special Vaal outcomes
- Separate from the prefix/suffix system

## Prefixes & Suffixes — Quick Reference

### Common Prefixes
**Weapons:** % increased damage, flat damage, % spell damage, % spirit, accuracy
**Armour/Jewellery:** +life, +mana, +spirit, % armour/evasion/ES, % movement speed (boots only)

### Common Suffixes
**Weapons:** attack/cast speed, attributes, crit chance/bonus, +level of skills, leech
**Armour/Jewellery:** resistances (fire/cold/lightning/chaos), regen, attributes

## Modifier Tiers

- T1 = highest/best tier, higher numbers = worse
- Each tier has a stat range (e.g., T1 Life on gloves = 120-149)
- Tier availability depends on item level
- Cannot reroll outside tier range — a T5 mod (80-89 life) will always be 80-89
- Divine Orb only rerolls within the existing tier range
- Different item types have different numbers of tiers for the same mod

### Key Item Level Breakpoints
- ilvl 82: T1 elemental resistances (+41-45%), T1 movement speed
- ilvl 81: T1 chaos resistance
- ilvl 60-70: Most T1 damage and defence mods
- ilvl 82+: Access to all tiers for most mods

## Local vs Global Modifiers

### Local Modifiers
- Affect the base stats on THAT specific item
- "% increased Physical Damage" on a weapon increases that weapon's base damage
- "% increased Energy Shield" on gloves increases that item's ES
- Indicated by blue text on base stats when affected

### Global Modifiers
- Affect your character's overall stats
- "+50 to Maximum Life" adds to your total life pool
- "+10 to Dexterity" increases your total dexterity
- "% increased Global Critical Hit Chance" applies to all attacks/spells

## Flat, Percent, and Hybrid Modifiers

### Flat
- A fixed amount: "+50 to Maximum Life", "Adds 11-19 Physical Damage"
- Provides a base that percentage increases scale from

### Percent (%)
- "65% increased Physical Damage" — multiplies existing values
- Stacks additively with other "increased" sources

### Hybrid
- Single mod that provides two stats at reduced values
- Example: "15-19% increased Physical Damage + 16-20 to Accuracy Rating"
- Takes only ONE mod slot despite showing two effects
- Cannot have two mods from the same family, but hybrid + non-hybrid of same stat IS allowed
- Values shown on item combine hybrid + non-hybrid visually

**Key implication for crafting:** To maximize a stat, you want flat + percent + hybrid versions, all as high tier as possible. They stack and only occupy 3 prefix slots total.

## Tags

- Almost all mods have associated tags (Life, Fire, Attack, Caster, etc.)
- Tags determine which mod pools and crafting methods can target them
- Visible by pressing Alt while hovering over tier numbers
- Used by: Essences, Catalysts, Omens, Influence Runes
- Understanding tags helps with targeted crafting outcomes

## Runes & Sockets

### Socket System
- Body Armour / 2H Martial Weapons: up to 2 sockets
- Gloves / Boots / Helmets / Shields / Focus / 1H Martial: up to 1 socket
- Quivers / Caster Weapons / Jewellery / Belts: CANNOT have sockets
- Artificer's Orb adds a socket
- Vaal Orb can add an extra socket beyond the normal limit

### Runes
- Insert into open sockets for permanent enchantments
- Effect differs based on weapon vs armour slot
- CANNOT be removed or replaced once socketed
- Example: Glacial Rune on weapon = adds cold damage; on armour = +cold resistance

### Soul Cores (Endgame)
- Like Runes but stronger and with wider mod options
- From Trials of Chaos (Ultimatum mechanic)
- Same socket rules as Runes — permanent once placed

## Practical Advice

- Don't use Divine Orbs on low-tier mods (waste of currency)
- Press Alt to see prefix/suffix labels and tier numbers
- Check item level before crafting — low ilvl = limited tier access
- Hybrid mods visually combine with non-hybrid versions — check Alt info
- Int armour rolls ES mods, Str rolls Armour mods, Dex rolls Evasion mods
- The correct attribute base is essential for getting desired mods
